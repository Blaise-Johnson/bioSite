Dear Diary...

Brianna is not your stereotypical middle child of three. Instead of falling victim to the middle child syndrome, her big personality almost demands attention everywhere she goes. She is only 5 feet and 5 inches but if you ask anyone who knows her, they'll say she's 6 feet at the very least. She was born February 19th, making her the water sign Pisces. Brianna is a self-proclaimed earthy spiritual person and doesn't fully believe that the personality traits of a Pisces match her very well. A Pisces is said to be compassionate, artistic, intuitive, gentle, wise and musical. She says she has a hard exterior and doesn't allow herself to present as gentle as a way of protecting herself. Growing up in a city outside of Dallas, Texas, Brianna had what she describes as a pretty average middle-class upbringing. Although she admits to experiencing middle child syndrome during her childhood, things have changed as she and her siblings have gotten older. She once felt that her younger sibling was the golden child, while her older brother received most of the attention when he joined the military. Now, she finds herself being the sibling who interacts with their parents the most. Some of Brianna's hobbies have stayed with her since childhood, including roller skating, doodling, and playing flag football. More recently, she has taken an interest in aerial yoga, sewing and caring for plants, which she jokingly says are surviving so far. Brianna has dreams of moving to Chicago someday and pursuing her love of fashion. Over the years she's traveled to many states and countries, but Chicago has a special place in her heart. Although she hasn't quite figured out exactly what she wants to do, she is confident that her personality and interests will help her find her way.
